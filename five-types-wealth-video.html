Understanding the 5 financial stages
every salaried person must know
Stage 1 of 5
Survival
Age: 25 – 30 Years
  • Whatever you earn, you spend
  • Unable to save much money
  • Income barely covers expenses
  • Still building the foundation
Stage 2 of 5
Security
Age: 30 – 35 Years
  • Getting married, expenses rising
  • Income growing, life stabilizing
  • More financially secure than before
  • Not yet ready for early retirement
Stage 3 of 5
Stability
Age: 35 – 45 Years
  • Income growing well
  • House purchased, loan manageable
  • Children in good schools
  • Regular investments in place
Investment Corpus
1× – 2× Annual Salary
Stage 4 of 5
Freedom
Age: 45 – 55 Years
  • Income very strong
  • Home fully paid off
  • Can quit job & live on passive income
  • Travel, pursue passions freely
Investment Corpus
5× – 8× Annual Salary
Stage 5 of 5
Abundance
Age: 60+ Years
  • Massive wealth accumulated
  • Multiple businesses or offices
  • Support charitable causes
  • Create positive change in society
Investment Corpus
10× – 20×+ Annual Salary
