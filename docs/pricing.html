Questions, answered

The things every owner asks before switching their front desk to AI.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Your agent is trained on your tone, your services, and your rules. Customers routinely thank it by name. And anything it isn't sure about gets handed to you instead of guessed.

Do I need new software or a new phone number?

No. TextLyra works with your existing WhatsApp Business number and can add a local SMS line. You keep using your phone exactly as you do today.

What if the AI can't answer something?

It says so honestly, takes the customer's details, and notifies you immediately with the full conversation. You reply once; it learns for next time.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within 48 hours of their onboarding call. Founding members get white-glove setup — we do everything.

Is there a contract?

Month to month, cancel anytime. If it doesn't pay for itself in bookings in the first 30 days, we'll refund the month.

Do you only work with businesses in Surrey?

We're based in Surrey, BC and serve all of Metro Vancouver — but everything is set up remotely, so we work with businesses anywhere in Canada and the US. Founding pricing applies wherever you are.
